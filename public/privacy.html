Privacy

The short version

This site has no analytics, no advertising pixels, and no third-party trackers. The only information collected is what you type into the sign-up form, and it is used for exactly the thing you signed up for.

What is stored

  • Your email address, and your name if you gave one.
  • Which option you picked: the book, working together, or both.
  • Your organization and message, if you filled those in.
  • Where the visit came from — a referring site or campaign tag.
  • The country your request came from, as reported by Cloudflare.
  • A one-way hash of your IP address and your browser's user-agent string, used only to stop automated abuse of the form. The raw IP is never written down.

Where it lives

In a Cloudflare D1 database attached to this site. It is not sold, rented, or shared. It is not fed into an advertising platform.

Getting removed

Every email carries a one-click unsubscribe link. You can also write to hello@givegetgrateful.com and ask for your record to be deleted outright — no reason needed, and it will be done rather than argued with.
