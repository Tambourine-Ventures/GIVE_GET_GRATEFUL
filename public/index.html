The idea

Three moves, in order

Most advice about generosity stops at "be generous." That's a value, not a method. This is the method.

  1. 01

    Give

    Start with something specific and costly enough to matter — your attention, your network, credit you could have kept. Vague goodwill doesn't compound. Particular acts do.

  2. 02

    Get

    Receiving well is the half everyone skips. People who can't ask, or can't accept, quietly run out — and then call it burnout. Getting is what makes the giving sustainable.

  3. 03

    Grateful

    Gratitude isn't the warm feeling at the end. It's the mechanism that closes the loop and starts the next one — which is why it belongs in your operating rhythm, not your journal.
